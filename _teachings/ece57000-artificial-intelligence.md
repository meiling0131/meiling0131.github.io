I am a **Teaching Assistant** for ECE 57000 in Fall 2026. My responsibilities include holding

## About the Course

ECE 57000 is Purdue's **graduate-level** introduction to artificial intelligence, taught by
[Prof. David I. Inouye](https://www.davidinouye.com/). The course covers the core ideas behind
modern AI systems and how to work with them effectively.

For the syllabus, schedule, lecture materials, and announcements, please refer to the official
course website:

- [ECE 57000 — Fall 2026](https://www.davidinouye.com/course/ece57000-fall-2026/)
